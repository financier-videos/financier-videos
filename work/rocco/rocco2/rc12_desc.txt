A "bank" that shows up in person to collect a fee? That's not your bank. 
Real banks don't text, call or knock to demand a fee or a payment on the spot. If someone says "I'm your bank, you owe a fee" - hang up or close the door, then call the number printed on your card.

Sources: FTC consumer advice on bank impersonation scams.
