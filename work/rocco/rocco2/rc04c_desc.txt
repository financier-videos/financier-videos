Would you click it? "Free GTA 6 for PC" is bait. GTA VI launched on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only - there is no PC version, so any PC download, "crack" or "free key" is fake. Security researchers have found fake GTA 6 downloads and "demo" sites that install password-stealing malware.
Protect yourself: buy only in the official PlayStation or Xbox store, never run a file a stranger, video or comment tells you to download, and turn on two-step verification.
