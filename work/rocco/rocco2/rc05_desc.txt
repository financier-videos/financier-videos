"Read me the code we just texted you." That's the whole scam. A caller says he's from your bank's fraud department, then asks for the one-time code your bank just sent - the code that lets him into your account.
Your move: never read a verification code to anyone who calls you. Hang up and call the number on the back of your card.

Sources: FTC consumer advice on verification-code scams; bank and card-issuer fraud guidance (they never call to ask for your one-time passcode).
