A box you never ordered, with nothing inside but a QR code? Don't scan it. Scammers mail "mystery" packages so the code sends you to a fake site that steals your login or payment info.
Your move: don't scan it, keep the box, and change your online shopping passwords.

Sources: FTC consumer alert on unordered packages with QR codes (brushing scams).
