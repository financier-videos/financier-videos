Someone sent you $1,000 "by mistake" and now wants it back? Don't send it.
The scam: they pay you with a stolen card or hacked account, then ask you to "return" it. The real owner reports the payment, the bank takes the $1,000 back from you, and the money you sent them is gone. You end up down $1,000.
Your move: send nothing back yourself. Report it in your payment app (or call your bank) and let them reverse it.

Sources: FTC consumer guidance on fake overpayment and "accidental payment" scams; Cash App and Zelle help pages on payments received by mistake.
