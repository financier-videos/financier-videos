Black Friday "90% off" store? Count the red flags: a website name that tries too hard, a countdown timer that pushes you to hurry, and a checkout that only takes gift cards. Real stores take credit cards.
Your move: pay with a credit card, and type the store's address yourself instead of clicking an ad or a link.

Sources: FTC and Better Business Bureau guidance on fake online stores and gift-card payment scams.
