Seen a "free GTA 6 key"? That's the bait. Fake key giveaways, "cracks" and early-access downloads are being used to steal gaming and email logins. Real GTA VI copies and keys come only from the official PlayStation or Xbox store.
Protect yourself: never type your password into a "free key" site, never run a file from a comment or video link, and turn on two-step verification.

Sources: Malwarebytes, "Fake GTA 6 Extended Look and demo sites deliver an infostealer" (Aug 2026); NordVPN and McAfee GTA 6 scam warnings.
