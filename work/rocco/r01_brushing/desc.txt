Would you keep it, or toss it? A box you never ordered is often a "brushing" scam: sellers ship to real addresses, then post fake reviews in your name.
The FTC warns: don't scan a QR code that comes with it - it can lead to a phishing site. Change your online shopping passwords. Legally, you don't have to pay for or return unordered items, but be careful using them.
