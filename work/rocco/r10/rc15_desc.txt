USPS 'redelivery fee' text with a link: scam. 'Verify your login or your account closes today': scam. Mom asking for pie: real.
USPS doesn't text you a link to pay a fee. A link + money or a deadline = scam.

Sources: USPS smishing guidance; FTC consumer advice on text scams.
