Seen a "GTA 6 for PC" download? GTA VI launched on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. There is no PC version, so any PC download, "crack" or "free key" claiming to be the game is fake. Security researchers have found fake GTA 6 downloads and "demo" sites that install password-stealing malware.
Protect yourself: buy only inside the official PlayStation or Xbox store, never run a file a video or comment tells you to download, and turn on two-step verification.

Sources: Rockstar Games (platforms, Nov 2026); Malwarebytes, "Fake GTA 6 Extended Look and demo sites deliver an infostealer" (Aug 2026); McAfee and NordVPN GTA 6 scam warnings.
