Should the nickel go next? A nickel costs 13.78 cents to make, a dime 5.76, a quarter 14.68, and the penny cost 3.69 before it was retired (U.S. Mint, fiscal 2024).
The last circulating penny was struck on Nov 12, 2025. "About 9 cents lost per nickel" is our math (13.78 − 5).

Sources:
• U.S. Mint 2024 Annual Report — fiscal 2024 unit costs (reported by Coin World and CoinNews, Feb 2025)
• Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, "When a Penny Costs More Than a Penny" (May 2026)
