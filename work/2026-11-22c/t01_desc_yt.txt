What would you do with one second of it?
GTA VI made an estimated $3.79 billion in retail sales on day one (Take-Two estimate, data through Nov 19, 2026). Sales, not profit: stores and platforms take their cut. GTA V made $800 million on its first day in 2013.
Our math (approximate): $3.79B ÷ 86,400 s ≈ $43,866 per second. One-dollar bills end to end: 3.79B × 6.14 in ≈ 591,000 km (the Moon is ≈ 384,400 km away). Stacked: 3.79B × 0.0043 in ≈ 413 km (the ISS orbits at ≈ 400–420 km). Weight: ≈ 1 g per bill ≈ 3,800 t ≈ 8 jumbo jets at max takeoff (≈ 450 t). Counting one bill per second ≈ 120 years.
