Would you climb it or spend it?
Adobe expects Americans to spend a record $275.1 billion online this holiday season (Nov 1 - Dec 31, 2026; forecast). In $100 bills that's 2.75 billion notes. Stacked up: about 186.7 miles tall - past the clouds, about 34 Mount Everests, three times past the edge of space (62 miles), and about 3,000 tons of paper.
Math: $275.1B / $100 = 2.751B notes x 0.0043 in = 11.83M in = 186.7 mi; Everest 29,032 ft = 5.50 mi (33.9x); 1 note ~1 g -> ~2,751 metric tons (~3,030 US tons).

Sources: Adobe Digital Insights 2026 holiday forecast (Sept 28, 2026); Bureau of Engraving and Printing currency FAQ (note thickness 0.0043 in, weight about 1 gram).
