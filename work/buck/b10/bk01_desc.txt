If you stacked America's $40 trillion debt in $100 bills, the stack would be about 43,688 km tall - past the satellites in geostationary orbit (35,786 km) and about 107 times higher than the International Space Station (~408 km).
Our math: $40T / $100 = 400 billion bills x 0.10922 mm (0.0043 in per bill, BEP).

Sources: US Treasury via NPR (debt passed $40 trillion, Aug 18 2026); fiscal-year-end debt $40.17T (Sept 30 2026); Bureau of Engraving and Printing (bill thickness).
