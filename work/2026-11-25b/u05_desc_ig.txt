$50 in October → $100 in early November → "WAS $100, NOW $50!" on Black Friday. Same fifty bucks, new costume. (Our illustration, not a real store.)
FTC guide: the "was" price must be real, offered for a reasonably substantial period. Massachusetts: 28 of the last 90 days. WalletHub 2025: ~36% of 3,100 Black Friday items offered no savings.

Sources: FTC 16 CFR 233.1; MA 940 CMR 6.05; WalletHub (Nov 2025).
