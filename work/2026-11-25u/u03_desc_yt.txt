Driving, flying, or staying home this year?
AAA forecasts 81.5 million Americans will travel 50+ miles from home over Thanksgiving week (Nov 24–30, 2026) — about 0.1% fewer than last year, ending three straight record years. 72.9 million are expected to drive; 5.96 million to fly. Gas averaged about $3.82 a gallon on Nov 16 vs $3.08 a year earlier. Domestic round trips booked through AAA average about $800, up 17%.
These are forecasts, not final numbers.
Our math (approximate): 81.5M ÷ ~340M U.S. residents ≈ 1 in 4. Holding hands: 81.5M × ~1.5 m ≈ 122,000 km ≈ 3 times around Earth (≈ 40,075 km). 121,000 fewer travelers = AAA's year-over-year difference.

Sources:
• AAA Thanksgiving Travel Forecast, Nov 2026 (via Fox Weather, ABC3340, Lodging Magazine)
• AAA gas prices, Nov 16, 2026 (via WFSB / Gray TV stations, Nov 17, 2026)
