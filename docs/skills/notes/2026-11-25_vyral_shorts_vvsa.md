# VVSA va Engaged Views diagnostikasi (2026-11-25)
Manba: github.com/vyralcontent/content-skills — skills/viral-youtube-shorts/SKILL.md (MIT, (c) 2026 Vyral). Kod yo'q, faqat markdown; kalit/akkaunt so'ramaydi. O'z so'zimiz bilan qisqa (matn ko'chirilmadi).

## Nima
Shorts flop bo'lganda taxmin emas, 4 o'lchov bo'yicha tashxis:
1. VVSA (Viewed vs Swiped Away, Studio > Shorts > "Stayed to watch") — ≥70% maqsad. Past bo'lsa — muammo 1-kadr/1-jumla.
2. Retention egri chizig'i 0–3 s: 80% dan pastga tushsa — sekin vizual yoki kirish so'zi.
3. Average % viewed: >100% = loop ishlayapti; <70% = o'rtada "o'lik" joy (egri chiziqdagi pog'ona soniyasini top).
4. Views vs Engaged Views: View har ijroda sanaladi (loop shishiradi); YPP uchun faqat Engaged Views (1k obunachi + 10M Engaged Views / 90 kun).
Layk soni Shortsda kuchsiz signal — unga qarab xulosa chiqarma.

## Qachon
C ishida (results.md): 24 soatda <300 ko'rgan har Short uchun yuqoridagi 4 raqamni yoz (vidiq_channel_analytics_extended yoki Studio; bo'lmasa "ma'lumot yo'q").

## Qadamlar
- results.md ga ustunlar: VVSA | 0–3 s retention | avg % viewed.
- VVSA <70% → hook uslubi playbook "TO'XTAT"; avg % viewed >100% → loop/oxir uslubi "IKKI BARAVAR".
- Retention pog'onasi soniyasini spec sahnasi bilan solishtir (qaysi sahna "o'ldirdi").

## Cheklov
Raqamlar (70%, 80%) — muallif evristikasi, gipoteza; o'z kanalimiz natijasi ustun. Studio ma'lumotiga vidIQ orqali kirish har doim ham yo'q.
